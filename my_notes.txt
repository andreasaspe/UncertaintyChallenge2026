Other notes:
Each targets a specific way locations differ: the crop stops the model memorizing "big centered animal" and forces scale-invariance, the jitter covers per-camera exposure/white-balance, and the grayscale covers the fact that camera traps switch to infrared at night. I used scale=(0.6, 1.0) rather than ImageNet's usual 0.08 deliberately — aggressive cropping would routinely cut the animal out of frame and hand the model a mislabeled image.
